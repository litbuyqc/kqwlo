03 / A practical browsing routine

From discovery to decision.

Four simple steps make product research easier to revisit and compare.

STEP / 01

Choose a category.

Begin with the piece you need: sneakers, a jacket, a jersey or an accessory. Narrow the search before comparing products.

STEP / 02

Read the listing.

Check the selected variant, seller description and photos. A search result title may leave out details that affect your choice.

STEP / 03

Check the fit.

Compare measurements with something you already own. Size labels alone are a weak basis for deciding whether an item will fit.

STEP / 04

Calculate the total.

Include delivery and any applicable fees. Recheck availability and terms before placing an order.

04 / Clear answers

A little clarity
before you browse.

Quick answers about spreadsheet research, product checks and where this page's links lead.

What is an Oopbuy spreadsheet?

An Oopbuy spreadsheet is a shopping research list used to organize product links and comparison notes. Useful fields include category, seller, listed price, size information, photo references and the date each link was checked.

Does this page provide a downloadable spreadsheet?

No. This page provides a browsing and comparison guide. Its category buttons open CAFANS pages; it does not host a downloadable workbook or an official Oopbuy product feed.

How should I check a product before ordering?

Open the current product listing, confirm the selected variant, compare garment or shoe measurements, review available photos and verify the seller's stated terms. A spreadsheet entry alone does not verify product quality or authenticity.

Does a listed product price include shipping?

Do not assume so. Check item price, domestic delivery, service fees, international delivery and any applicable taxes or duties separately. Use the current checkout or shipping quote for the actual destination.

Are brand searches proof of authenticity or availability?

No. Nike, Louis Vuitton, Gucci and Hermès are brand search references. Brand names, photos and spreadsheet labels do not establish authenticity, authorization or current stock.

Is this an official Oopbuy website?

No. This is an independent discovery guide directing visitors to CAFANS. It is not an official Oopbuy service and does not claim affiliation with the brands mentioned.
